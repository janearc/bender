two tables from the colour & vision research laboratory at ucl,
http://www.cvrl.org, which keeps the cie's standard data in plain csv.

    ciexyz31_1.csv   the cie 1931 2-degree observer, 360 to 830 nm at 1 nm:
                     wavelength, x-bar, y-bar, z-bar. how strongly each
                     wavelength counts toward X, Y and Z.
    d65.csv          cie standard illuminant d65, 300 to 830 nm at 1 nm:
                     wavelength, relative power. average daylight.

they are data, not code: never edited, only replaced from the source.
